Click a guest, then click a seat  ·  or drag a name onto a seat  ·  drag seat→seat to swap  ·  right-click a seat to lock/unlock. Tap Guest list, pick a name, then tap a seat  ·  tap a filled seat for their details, Lock or Remove  ·  + to zoom in far enough to tap comfortably. White = empty, black = filled, gold = locked.
